1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how Sparkstr ("Sparkstr", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the Sparkstr mobile application and related services ("App").

Sparkstr helps users save, organise, analyse, search, and rediscover videos and related information.

For privacy questions or requests, contact: contact@sparkstr.com.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Anonymous account information

Sparkstr automatically creates an anonymous Firebase account so that features such as Sparks, saved processing jobs, recommendations, notifications, and subscriptions can work.

This account has a unique Firebase user identifier ("UID"). Sparkstr does not require your name, email address, telephone number, or social-media account to create this account.

Although the account is described as anonymous, its UID is a persistent technical identifier. It allows Sparkstr to recognise the same account and associate relevant records with it. This is pseudonymous information, rather than necessarily anonymous information under data-protection law. Sparkstr does not intentionally connect that UID to your real-world identity unless you provide identifying information yourself, for example in feedback or a save reason.

2.2 Content you provide

Information you choose to provide may include:

  • Video links and content links you save or share with Sparkstr
  • An optional reason explaining why you saved a video
  • Folder names, tags, categories, key points, and other organisation information
  • Search terms and interactions used to operate search and recommendation features
  • Videos or media files you choose to import directly
  • Feedback, support messages, and an optional contact email address

Notes and your downloaded vault media are generally stored locally on your device. Information needed to process a video, including its link and save reason, may be sent to Sparkstr's backend services.

Providing a save reason or a support email address is optional. If you do not provide a link or media needed for a requested processing feature, Sparkstr cannot carry out that request.

2.3 Generated video information

Sparkstr may create or store information generated from a saved video or link, including:

  • Titles and summaries
  • Key points, tags, keywords, folders, and categories
  • Thumbnails, screenshots, transcripts, or related media information
  • Recommendations, matching signals, and semantic representations
  • Processing status, errors, and technical job information

2.4 Device, technical, and usage information

Depending on the features you use, Sparkstr and its providers may process:

  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Account, installation, or device-level identifiers
  • Timezone and approximate location derived from technical information
  • IP address and network information
  • App opens, screens viewed, and feature interactions
  • Crash, diagnostic, performance, and security information

2.5 Analytics

Sparkstr may use services such as Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used and to improve its features, performance, reliability, and user experience.

Analytics may include app opens, screen views, feature usage, app version, device information, operating-system information, approximate location, and diagnostic or usage events.

Sparkstr does not intentionally send the contents of locally stored notes, full saved videos, feedback messages, or full search-query text to Firebase Analytics.

2.6 Feedback and support

You may send feedback, report an issue, or suggest improvements. Providing an email address is optional, but we may be unable to reply if you do not provide one.

To prevent abuse, diagnose problems, and understand which account submitted a report, a feedback submission may include your Firebase UID, source IP address, app version, device or platform information, and the contents of your message.

Please do not include sensitive or unnecessary personal information in feedback, save reasons, folder names, or other free-text fields.

2.7 Notifications

If you enable notifications, Sparkstr may process a push-notification token, platform information, notification preferences, delivery information, and interactions with notifications.

Sparkstr uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to support push notifications. On iOS, delivery also uses Apple Push Notification service ("APNs"). Firebase may associate an APNs device token with an FCM registration token so that notifications can reach the correct app installation. Sparkstr's backend stores notification registration information associated with your account.

You can disable notifications through Sparkstr or your device settings. Notification previews may appear on your lock screen depending on your device settings.

2.8 Advertising

Current versions of Sparkstr on Android and iOS do not display advertisements or offer rewarded ads. Sparkstr does not use Google AdMob in these versions and does not collect advertising identifiers for personalised advertising or cross-app advertising tracking.

2.9 Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play on Android and Apple's App Store on iOS. Sparkstr may receive purchase identifiers, product information, transaction status, subscription status, and entitlement information.

On iOS, this can include signed transaction information, transaction and original transaction identifiers, an app account token used to associate a purchase with an account, purchase and expiry dates, renewal status, and information about billing grace periods, cancellations, refunds, and revocations.

Sparkstr uses this information to verify purchases, restore subscriptions, manage access to Premium, and maintain the correct Sparks allowance. Store transaction identifiers may continue to identify the same purchase after reinstalling the App or creating a new anonymous Firebase account.

Sparkstr does not receive or store your complete payment-card details.

4. Local and Cloud Storage

Sparkstr uses a combination of local device storage and cloud services.

  • Vault media, local notes, folders, settings, and related information may be stored in the App's private storage on your device.
  • Account profiles, Sparks, subscription status, notification information, processing jobs, generated metadata, and recommendation information may be stored using Firebase, Google Cloud, or other backend providers.
  • Video links, save reasons, and information needed to analyse or organise content may be transmitted to cloud processing services.
  • Apple subscription records and remaining Sparks allowances may be stored separately from the Firebase user profile, using an identifier derived from the original Apple purchase. This allows restoration without issuing the same paid-period allowance more than once.

Removing the App or clearing its storage may delete locally stored content. Cloud information is handled separately as described below.

5. Video Processing and Artificial Intelligence

When you submit a supported video, media file, or link, Sparkstr may send the content or link to backend, video-processing, extraction, and artificial intelligence providers.

These services may be used to retrieve supported content and generate titles, summaries, transcripts, key points, tags, folders, thumbnails, screenshots, recommendations, and other metadata.

Providers may include Google Firebase and Google Cloud, OpenAI, ScrapeCreators, and other infrastructure or content-processing providers used to deliver the requested feature. Information sent for processing may include your submitted save reason where it is used to personalise the requested analysis.

AI-generated information may be inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated. Sparkstr's output should not be treated as professional, medical, legal, or financial advice.

6. Your Choices and Consent

You can choose whether to provide optional save reasons, submit feedback, supply a support email address, or enable notifications.

You can turn off notifications through Sparkstr's notification settings or your device settings. To withdraw consent for other processing that relies on consent, contact contact@sparkstr.com.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that occurred lawfully before consent was withdrawn. It also does not automatically stop processing that has another lawful basis, such as keeping records required by law.

Current versions of Sparkstr do not offer advertising or advertising personalisation, so advertising consent is not required to earn or replenish Sparks.

7. Purchases and Subscriptions

Google Play and Apple's App Store process purchases and subscriptions under their own terms and privacy policies.

Sparkstr verifies subscription information with the relevant store. On iOS, Apple's signed transactions and server notifications help us recognise renewals, cancellations, expiry, refunds, and other changes affecting your subscription.

Restoring an Apple subscription may associate the original purchase and its remaining allowance with your current anonymous Firebase account. This does not necessarily restore locally stored videos, notes, or other vault content.

Deleting your Sparkstr account does not automatically cancel an active store subscription. You should manage or cancel subscriptions through Google Play or Apple's App Store.

8. When We Share Information

We do not sell personal information for money or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising in current versions of Sparkstr.

We may disclose information to service providers where necessary to operate Sparkstr, including:

  • Firebase and Google Cloud for authentication, storage, backend services, analytics, and notifications
  • Apple and Google for app distribution, purchases, subscriptions, and notification delivery
  • OpenAI and other artificial-intelligence processing providers
  • ScrapeCreators and other supported-content processing providers
  • Email and support providers used to receive and respond to feedback
  • Hosting, security, monitoring, and technical infrastructure providers

Sparkstr may use saved-content metadata to provide recommendations to other users. Content you submit may also contain information about you or other people, so avoid including unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect legal rights, investigate fraud or abuse, protect users, or respond to valid legal requests.

9. International Transfers

Some service providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States or other locations where they operate.

Where required, international transfers are protected using appropriate safeguards, such as adequacy regulations, contractual protections, or standard contractual clauses provided by the relevant service provider. You can contact us for information about the safeguards applicable to your information.

10. Retention

We retain information according to the following criteria:

  • Anonymous account and profile information is kept while the account remains active or until the account is deleted, subject to any applicable legal retention requirements.
  • Notification registration information is kept as needed to deliver enabled notifications and manage delivery. Registrations stored under your profile are removed when that profile is deleted. Providers may separately retain technical records under their own retention settings.
  • Processing jobs and generated video metadata may be kept while needed to provide Sparkstr, support recommendations, prevent duplicate processing, maintain the content service, resolve technical problems, and protect the service from abuse.
  • When an account is deleted, Sparkstr removes the UID from matching job and recommendation-index records. The remaining video metadata and save reason may be retained without that account UID. Removing the UID does not make information anonymous if the remaining content identifies someone.
  • Feedback and support messages are kept while reasonably needed to respond, investigate the issue, maintain support history, and protect against abuse. Feedback stored only in deleted user-owned subcollections is removed with those subcollections; separate support correspondence may remain subject to these retention criteria.
  • Apple purchase identifiers, subscription status, and the associated allowance record may remain separately from the user profile after account deletion. These records support subscription restoration, verification, and prevention of duplicate paid-period allowances. The link from the deleted Firebase account to that subscription record is removed.
  • Purchase, transaction, security, and legal records may be retained for applicable legal obligations, store requirements, legitimate dispute handling, and proportionate fraud prevention. Relevant criteria include whether a subscription or restoration entitlement continues, whether a refund or dispute remains possible or unresolved, and whether retention is legally required.
  • Provider logs, backups, and diagnostic records are retained according to applicable provider settings and backup cycles before being overwritten or deleted.

Retained purchase and technical identifiers are not necessarily anonymous. Retention must remain necessary and proportionate to its purpose and is subject to applicable privacy rights and legal obligations. You can contact us about a particular record or retention period.

Information may be retained for longer where necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims, comply with law, or investigate abuse, where permitted by applicable law.

11. Account Deletion

You can initiate account deletion inside Sparkstr using the Delete Account option in Settings.

Deleting the account will:

  • Delete the anonymous Firebase Authentication account
  • Delete the account's cloud profile and user-owned subcollections
  • Remove notification registrations and account-specific preferences stored in that profile
  • Remove the deleted UID from matching processing jobs and recommendation-index records
  • Remove the stored link between that Firebase account and its separate Apple subscription record

Sparkstr preserves the remaining processing-job and generated video information, including a save reason where one exists, but removes the deleted account UID from those records. This is not a guarantee that the remaining content contains no personal information.

If a retained save reason, video, or other record contains information identifying you, you may request deletion or correction by contacting contact@sparkstr.com. Please provide enough information to identify the relevant record, without sending unnecessary sensitive information.

Separate purchase, allowance, security, and legal records may remain as described in section 10. Deleting a profile therefore does not mean that every transaction or technical record has been erased.

Account deletion does not delete the vault stored locally on your device. You can remove local information by clearing the App's data, using available local deletion controls, or uninstalling the App. Copies held in device backups may need to be managed separately through your device or backup provider.

After deletion, Sparkstr may create a new anonymous account if you continue using or reopen the App. The new account will have a different UID. Restoring an existing Apple purchase may reconnect its subscription entitlement and remaining allowance to this new account.

Account deletion does not automatically cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Unless cancelled through the relevant store, an auto-renewing subscription may continue to renew and be charged.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of information
  • Request restriction of certain processing
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Request a portable copy of eligible information
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

These rights may be subject to legal exceptions. Because Sparkstr primarily uses pseudonymous technical identifiers, we may need your UID, job identifier, relevant purchase reference, feedback email address, or other information to locate the relevant records and reasonably verify your request.

Please do not send payment-card details, account passwords, authentication tokens, or other unnecessary sensitive information with a privacy request.

To exercise a privacy right, contact contact@sparkstr.com.

If you are in the United Kingdom, you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/ . You do not have to contact us before exercising that right.

Sparkstr may use automated systems to generate summaries, categorise saved content, recommend videos, and decide when to resurface content.

These features are intended to organise and personalise the App. They are not intended to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

No application, network, server, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information into save reasons, feedback, folders, or other free-text fields.

If you believe a child has provided information through Sparkstr, contact us at contact@sparkstr.com.
