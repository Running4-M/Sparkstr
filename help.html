Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my response not appearing?
If you create an event that requires AI support and schedule it for the same day, please allow about one minute for the response to be generated. In most cases responses appear quickly, but same-day events may need a brief processing period.
Why is there not a free trial?
Sparkstr does not offer a separate free trial because the Free plan already gives you access to almost all features available in the higher tiers. The main limitations are that you cannot add URLs, files, or images on the Free plan. While usage is more restricted compared to paid plans, you can still experience the core functionality and see how Sparkstr works for you before deciding to upgrade. This way, you get a real feel for the app—no credit card required!
Can I get a refund if I cancel my subscription?
At this time, Sparkstr does not offer refunds. Subscriptions are non-refundable once billed. However, we are actively reviewing this policy and may adjust it in the near future. Please stay tuned for updates on our Reddit community, where we will share all policy changes.
How do I cancel my plan?
You can cancel your subscription at any time by switching to the Free tier in your account settings.

Please note: cancellations take effect immediately. If you cancel before the end of your billing cycle, you will not receive money back for unused days. For example, if you pay on the 1st of the month and cancel on the 20th, your account moves to Free instantly and no refund is given for the remaining days.

We recommend waiting until just before your renewal date if you wish to maximize the value of your paid plan.
Can I switch between Basic and Pro plans?
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time, and changes apply immediately.

We use prorated billing: This means that if you’ve already paid for part of your current plan, you won’t lose that money — it’s credited toward your new plan.

Example 1 (Upgrading Basic → Pro):
- Basic Plan: £2.49 per month (≈ £0.08 per day).
- You paid for Basic 5 days ago → about £0.40 used, leaving £2.09 unused.
- Pro Plan: £4.99 per month (≈ £0.17 per day). With 25 days left, the Pro portion costs about £4.15.
- Stripe applies your £2.09 unused Basic as credit toward that £4.15.
- You only pay the difference: £4.15 – £2.09 = £2.06 today.
- Next cycle: full £4.99/month.

Example 2 (Downgrading Pro → Basic):
- Pro Plan: £4.99 per month (≈ £0.17 per day).
- You paid for Pro 5 days ago → about £0.85 used, leaving £4.14 unused.
- Basic Plan: £2.49 per month (≈ £0.08 per day). With 25 days left, the Basic portion costs about £2.09.
- Stripe uses your £4.14 unused Pro credit to cover the £2.09 Basic cost.
- The extra credit (£2.05) will be applied to your next month’s invoice.
- Next cycle: full £2.49/month.

Note: This does not apply when moving from a paid plan to the Free plan. In that case, your subscription is simply cancelled and no credit or refund is provided.

For more details, see contact us at contact@sparkstr.com

What happens if I delete my account?
Deleting your account from the settings will permanently remove all of your information, including events, Smart Plans, chat history, and documents. Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered. Please be certain before proceeding.
