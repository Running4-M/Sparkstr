Frequently Asked Questions

How do notifications work?
At the moment, you will only receive notifications for your events if you have installed our web app (PWA) on your device. Make sure to install Sparkstr from your browser’s menu to enable notifications. We are working on expanding notification support in the future!
Why is my response not appearing?
If you create an event that requires AI support and schedule it for the same day, please allow about one minute for the response to be generated. In most cases responses appear quickly, but same-day events may need a brief processing period.
Why is there not a free trial?
Sparkstr does not offer a separate free trial because the Free plan already gives you access to almost all features available in the higher tiers. The main limitations are that you cannot add URLs, files, or images on the Free plan. While usage is more restricted compared to paid plans, you can still experience the core functionality and see how Sparkstr works for you before deciding to upgrade. This way, you get a real feel for the app—no credit card required!
Can I get a refund if I cancel my subscription?
Yes! If you cancel your paid subscription by downgrading to the Free plan, you will receive a prorated refund for the unused portion of your current billing period. The refund is automatically sent to your original payment method. It may take some time to appear, depending on your bank or payment provider.

If you have any issues or do not see your refund, please contact us at contact@sparkstr.com and we’ll be happy to help.
Can I switch between Basic and Pro plans?
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time, and changes apply immediately.

Refunds for downgrades: If you downgrade from Pro to Basic (or from any paid plan to a lower-priced plan), you will receive a prorated refund for the unused portion of your previous plan. The refund is sent directly to your original payment method.

Example 1 (Upgrading Basic → Pro):
- You paid for Basic (£2.49/month) and upgrade to Pro (£4.99/month) halfway through the month.
- You are charged only for the remaining days of Pro, minus the unused value of your Basic plan.
- The system automatically calculates the difference and charges you accordingly.

Example 2 (Downgrading Pro → Basic):
- You paid for Pro (£4.99/month) and downgrade to Basic (£2.49/month) halfway through the month.
- You will receive a prorated refund for the unused days of your Pro plan, sent straight to your bank account or card.
- Your new Basic plan starts immediately.

Note: If you move from a paid plan to the Free plan, you will also receive a prorated refund for any unused days of your paid subscription.

For more details, contact us at contact@sparkstr.com.

What happens if I delete my account?
Deleting your account from the settings will permanently remove all of your information, including events, Smart Plans, chat history, and documents. Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered. Please be certain before proceeding.
