Sparkstr

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 27/03/2026

1. Introduction

Sparkstr is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, how it is stored, and what choices you have when using the Sparkstr mobile application ("App").

Sparkstr is designed to help users save, organise, analyse, search, and rediscover videos and related information.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Content You Provide

When you use Sparkstr, you may choose to provide content through the App. This may include:

  • Videos you upload, save, or share within the App
  • Links to videos or content you choose to save
  • Optional text associated with saved videos, such as notes or a save reason
  • Information generated from your saved videos, such as titles, summaries, key points, tags, folders, thumbnails, and related metadata

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

To provide the core features of Sparkstr, the App may collect or use limited technical information, including:

  • An anonymous user identifier through Firebase
  • Your timezone
  • Basic technical information needed for app functionality, syncing, or troubleshooting

The Firebase anonymous identifier is used to provide app functionality and is not linked to your real-world identity by Sparkstr.

2.3 Feedback and Support Messages

Sparkstr may allow you to send feedback, report issues, or suggest improvements through a feedback form.

Feedback can be submitted without providing your name or email address. If you want a reply, you may choose to provide an email address. Providing an email address is optional.

Important: Feedback submissions are not linked to your Firebase anonymous user identifier. When you send feedback, Sparkstr does not receive your Firebase ID as part of that feedback submission.

If you do not provide an email address, Sparkstr will not be able to reply to your feedback. However, feedback messages may still be reviewed to improve the App.

Please avoid including personal or sensitive information in feedback messages unless it is necessary for your request.

2.4 Advertising Data

Sparkstr may use third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob. These services may collect or use information such as:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device-level data
  • Ad interaction data
  • Information used to show personalised or non-personalised ads, depending on your choices and applicable law

3. How We Use Information

We use information to operate, improve, and support Sparkstr. This includes using information to:

  • Store and manage videos and related content within the App
  • Generate titles, summaries, key points, tags, folders, thumbnails, and other useful metadata
  • Provide search, folders, rediscovery, recommendations, and other core features
  • Sync app data where cloud services are used
  • Display advertisements
  • Manage subscriptions or purchases through Google Play
  • Review feedback and improve the App
  • Respond to feedback or support requests if you provide an email address
  • Detect, investigate, and fix bugs, errors, or technical issues

4. Data Storage

Sparkstr may store data locally on your device and, where applicable, in cloud services such as Firebase or other backend services used to provide app functionality.

  • Videos and related data may be stored locally on your device
  • Some data may be stored in Firebase cloud services where needed for app functionality
  • Processing jobs, generated metadata, and related app data may be associated with an anonymous identifier
  • Feedback messages are handled separately from the Firebase anonymous user identifier

Data associated with the Firebase anonymous identifier is not linked to your real-world identity by Sparkstr.

5. Video Processing and Generated Information

When you upload, share, or save a video, Sparkstr may process that video or related link to create useful information such as a title, summary, key points, tags, folder/category, thumbnail, save reason, and other metadata.

This processing is used to make your saved videos easier to search, organise, understand, and rediscover later.

Generated information may not always be complete or accurate, and you should review important information yourself before relying on it.

6. Advertising

Sparkstr may use third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob to display ads. These services may:

  • Show personalised or non-personalised ads
  • Use device identifiers and similar technologies
  • Measure ad performance and interactions

Users in regions such as the United Kingdom, European Economic Area, and other applicable regions may be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

7. Subscriptions and Payments

Subscriptions and payments are managed through the Google Play Store. Sparkstr does not collect or store your payment card details.

Google Play may process payment information according to Google’s own terms and privacy policies.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed to provide Sparkstr, support users, improve the App, comply with legal obligations, and protect the App from misuse.

  • Locally stored data may be removed if you uninstall the App or clear app data
  • Cloud-stored app data may remain unless deleted through the App or removed following a request
  • Feedback messages may be kept for as long as reasonably necessary to review the feedback, improve the App, or respond where requested
  • If you provided an email address for feedback, you can request deletion of that email address and related feedback message

For assistance with data removal, contact: contact@sparkstr.com

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data.

We may share limited information with service providers where necessary to operate Sparkstr, including:

  • Cloud and backend service providers used for storage, syncing, processing, or app functionality
  • Advertising providers for ad delivery and measurement
  • Google Play for subscriptions, purchases, and payment management
  • Analytics, crash reporting, or technical service providers if used to maintain and improve the App

These services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

10. Third-Party Platforms

Sparkstr may allow you to save, upload, or process content from third-party platforms such as YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook.

Sparkstr is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of those third-party platforms. Your use of third-party platforms remains subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by Sparkstr. However, no app, website, server, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should avoid submitting sensitive personal information through Sparkstr unless it is necessary.

12. Children’s Privacy

Sparkstr is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided information through Sparkstr, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

13. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights relating to your personal information. These may include the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection to certain processing.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us at: contact@sparkstr.com

Because Sparkstr mainly uses anonymous identifiers, we may need information from you to help locate the relevant data. For feedback messages, if you submitted an email address, please contact us from that same email address where possible.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in the App or made available through the same place where this policy is provided.

The effective date at the top of this page will show when this Privacy Policy was last updated.
