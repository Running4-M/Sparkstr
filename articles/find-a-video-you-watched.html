How to Find a Video You Watched on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube or Facebook

Lost a video you scrolled past? Step-by-step ways to find it again on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Facebook, what to try when it isn't in your history, and how to never lose one again.

By the Sparkstr teamUpdated 22 November 20267 min read

Quick answer

Every big app keeps some record of what you watched. Start with the app's watch history, then check your likes, saves and messages. If it isn't there, search using words someone said in the video. The table below shows where to look on each app.

AppWhere your watch history lives
TikTokProfile → ☰ menu → Settings and privacy → Activity center → Watch history
InstagramProfile → ☰ menu → Your activity → Watch history (Reels only, roughly the last 30 days)
YouTubeYou tab → History (or youtube.com/feed/history on a computer)
FacebookMenu → Settings & privacy → Activity log → Videos you've watched

Apps rename and move their menus often, so if a button isn't exactly where we say, look for the nearest match. The words "Activity" and "History" are nearly always in the path.

How to find a video you watched on TikTok

1. Check your watch history

  1. Tap Profile in the bottom right.
  2. Tap the ☰ menu in the top right, then Settings and privacy.
  3. Open Activity center, then Watch history.

You'll see a grid of videos you've watched, newest first. TikTok only keeps a limited window of history, so if the video was a while ago, go here first before it drops off.

Watch history empty? It can be switched off. Check the settings icon inside the Watch history screen and make sure it's turned on, so the next video you lose is easy to find.

2. Check your likes and favourites

On your profile, the heart tab shows videos you've liked, and the bookmark tab shows Favourites. If you double-tapped the video without thinking, it's probably here.

3. Search what you remember

TikTok's search reads captions, hashtags and often the words spoken in the video. Type the most unusual thing you remember, like "one pan garlic butter orzo" rather than "pasta recipe". Then use the filters to narrow by date or to videos you've watched before, if your app shows that option.

4. Follow the sound

If you remember the song or audio, search for it and tap through to the sound's page. It lists every video that used it.

How to find a Reel you watched on Instagram

1. Use Reels watch history

  1. Go to your Profile and tap the ☰ menu.
  2. Tap Your activity.
  3. Scroll down and tap Watch history.

Instagram added this in late 2025. It covers Reels only, reportedly from about the last 30 days. You can sort by date and filter by the account that posted, which helps if you remember who made it but not when.

2. Check likes, saved and comments

  • Liked: Your activity → Interactions → Likes
  • Commented: Your activity → Interactions → Comments
  • Saved: ☰ menu → Saved

3. Check your DMs

If you sent the Reel to a friend, it's still sitting in that chat. Open the conversation, tap the person's name at the top, and look for shared media or links.

4. Go to the creator's profile

If you remember the account, open their profile and tap the Reels tab. That's often faster than searching.

How to find a video you watched on YouTube

YouTube has the most useful history of the four, because you can search inside it.

  1. In the app, tap You in the bottom right, then History (or View all next to History).
  2. Tap the search icon at the top of your history and type a word from the title or channel.
  3. On a computer, go straight to youtube.com/feed/history and use the search box on the right.

Shorts appear in your history too. If nothing shows up at all, your watch history may be paused. You can check that in the same History page or in your Google account's YouTube History settings. Paused history can't be recovered for that period, so turn it back on now.

Also check: the Liked videos playlist and Watch later, both under the You tab.

How to find a video you watched on Facebook

  1. Tap the Menu (your profile picture or ☰).
  2. Go to Settings & privacy → Activity log.
  3. Look for Videos you've watched. You may need to tap Filter or scroll through the categories.

Saved posts and videos live under Menu → Saved. Reels you reacted to show up in the activity log under your interactions.

Not in your history? Try these

  • Search with Google. Type what you remember plus the site, for example site:tiktok.com garlic butter orzo. Search engines index lots of public posts.
  • Search a quote, not a topic. Spoken lines and on-screen text are far more unique than the subject.
  • Ask your friends. If you showed someone or sent it, it's probably in a chat.
  • Check another app. Creators often post the same video on TikTok, Reels, Shorts and Facebook. If one app's history fails, another might have it.
  • Request your data. TikTok, Instagram, Google and Facebook all let you download a copy of your account data, which can include watch history. It takes a while, but it's thorough.

Avoid third-party "history viewer" apps that ask for your password. None of them have special access, and handing over your login puts your account at risk.

How to never lose a video again

Watch history is a safety net, not a system. It expires, it's split across four apps, and it's full of things you didn't care about. The fix is to save the video at the moment it matters, in one place:

  1. Save it the second you think "I'll need this." If you wait, it's gone.
  2. Add one line on why. "Recipe for Sunday" or "gift idea for Mum" is what you'll actually search for later.
  3. Keep everything in one place instead of four separate Saved folders.

Frequently asked questions

Can I see a TikTok I watched but didn't like?

Yes, if Watch history is turned on. Go to Profile → ☰ → Settings and privacy → Activity center → Watch history. It includes videos you only watched, not just ones you liked.

Does Instagram have a watch history for Reels?

Yes. Since late 2025 you can find it under Profile → ☰ → Your activity → Watch history. It covers Reels, not regular photo posts, and only goes back a limited time.

How far back does YouTube history go?

YouTube keeps your history until you delete it or until your Google account's auto-delete setting removes it. You can check the auto-delete period in your Google account under YouTube History.

Can I find a video I watched while logged out?

Usually not through history, because it's tied to your account. Your best bet is searching for a quote or on-screen text, or a Google search with the site name.
